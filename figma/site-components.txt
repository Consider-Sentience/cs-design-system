This is the considersentience.ai site. Format: name [type WxH] layout (H/V, gap, padding top,right,bottom,left, alignment) fill/stroke/radius, then for text: "sample" font size/line-height, case, color.
Nav links: text and brackets are ink (#1B2430) at 55% opacity, so they sit on the cloud background.

Assets on this page: "square" (logo mark, 248x248), "full" (wordmark 168x27 / 174x28 / 124x20), "Favicon 1" (48x48), "Social image 1" (1200x630). Copies of the images are in ../assets.
